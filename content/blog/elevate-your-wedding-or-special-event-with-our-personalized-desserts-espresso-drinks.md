Looking to host an unforgettable wedding or party tailored to your theme or love story? At Blue Bird Mobile Dessert & Espresso Bar, we specialize in personalized experiences that will leave your guests swooning. From decadent desserts to elegant espresso drinks, we make sure every detail of your event is just right.

We're located north of Boston and travel all over New England, serving desserts and espresso drinks at weddings, corporate events, and private parties. We work closely with you to create a personalized, scratch-made dessert spread that matches your theme, preferences, and story. Whether you dream of an elegant dessert table or affogatos made with locally sourced ice cream, we bring your vision to life.

We've set up at elegant weddings in Boston, seaside weddings on Cape Cod, in Maine, Rhode Island, and along the Massachusetts coast. You might find us at a rustic barn wedding north of Boston, a historic house in New Hampshire or the Berkshires, a private home, or a venue overlooking the ocean.

## Two Ways to Set Up

Our personalized approach extends to the setup itself. Choose between our retro blue camper or our hand-built espresso cart for your event.

The **camper** features a lounge area where guests can relax, linger, and take photos — it's a destination within your event. For smaller or indoor events, **the Perch** espresso cart is the perfect size. Either way, our skilled baristas serve rich lattes and a variety of pastries, with a large selection of gluten-free and dairy-free options.

## What Sets Us Apart

We understand that your wedding day is one of the most important days of your life, and we go above and beyond to make sure every aspect of the dessert and espresso experience is flawless. From the initial consultation to the last sip of coffee, we work to exceed your expectations and create memories that last.
