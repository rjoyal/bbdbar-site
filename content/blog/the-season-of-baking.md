## Cinnamon Bun Wreath

This one uses refrigerated dough from Immaculate Baking Company, so the hard work is already done for you. The result looks beautiful on a holiday table.

### Ingredients

- 1–2 tubes of refrigerated Immaculate Baking Cinnamon or Gingerbread rolls (depending on guest count)
- Garnishes: orange peels, fresh cranberries, cinnamon sticks, sprinkles, fresh greenery

### Instructions

1. Bake the rolls according to the package directions.
2. Frost and arrange on a platter in a wreath shape.
3. Garnish as desired with orange peels, cranberries, cinnamon sticks, and greenery.
