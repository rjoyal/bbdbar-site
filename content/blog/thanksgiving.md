## Photo Place Cards

One of the most fun things you can do at a Thanksgiving table is scatter childhood photos. Collect pictures from family members beforehand and create place cards featuring each guest's younger self — baby photos for the little ones. Young and old guests love it equally.

## Individual Bread Loaves

Personal mini loaves of bread make a charming addition to any place setting. Tie each one with a piece of string, add a fresh sprig of thyme or rosemary, and tuck in a name tag — instant warmth and welcome. Disposable mini bread pans are available from Amazon and King Arthur Flour.

![Individual mini bread loaves](/photos/blog/thanksgiving/bread-loaves.jpeg)

### Mini Cheese Herb Breads

Makes 6 mini loaves.

**Ingredients:**
- 3½ cups flour
- ⅔ cup sugar
- 4 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1½ cups milk
- ½ cup cooking oil
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- ½ cup fresh thyme, parsley, and oregano, chopped

**Instructions:**
1. Grease mini loaf pans and preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, herbs, and 1 cup of the cheddar in a large bowl. Set aside.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the eggs, milk, and oil.
4. Make a well in the flour mixture and add the wet ingredients. Blend until just combined — lumps are fine.
5. Divide evenly among the 6 mini loaf pans. Top each with the remaining cheddar.
6. Bake for 25 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Tie each loaf with string and a sprig of fresh herbs. Add a name tag if using as a place setting.

## Gold Acorns, Leaves, and Pumpkins

A quick and beautiful tablescape touch: gather mini pumpkins, fallen leaves, and acorns and give them a light spray of gold paint. Arrange them down the center of the table for an easy, elegant seasonal display.
